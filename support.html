Questions, bug reports, or ideas — email axiomapplied365@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

The sky doesn't match what I see. Allow location access, then wave the phone in a figure-8 to calibrate the compass. Make sure you're not near large metal objects or magnets.

No stars in my star-trail photo. Street lights overpower faint stars. Try a darker spot, point at the sky (not buildings), and keep the phone perfectly still — a tripod helps a lot.

Restore my purchase. Settings (slider icon) ▸ Restore purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Refunds. All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple, not the developer: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. Apple decides per its policy, usually within a few days. If a refund is granted, Pro switches off in the app automatically.
